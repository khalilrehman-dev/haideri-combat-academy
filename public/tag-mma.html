Orthodox

A left-foot-forward alignment commonly used in boxing and other striking sports.

A coach can help you find a comfortable base rather than choosing only by your dominant hand.Compare orientation
striking02

Southpaw

A right-foot-forward alignment: the mirror of an orthodox stance.

The reversed lead side changes the relationship between two athletes. Learn it with supervised practice.Compare orientation
striking03

Switch stance

Moving between orthodox and southpaw rather than staying with one lead side.
